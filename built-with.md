## What I Built This With

I want to be transparent about every tool
I used to build this workflow.

No hidden tools. No expensive software.
Everything here is either free or very affordable.

---

### n8n — Workflow Automation

This is the brain of the whole system.

n8n connects all the tools together.
It listens to Telegram.
It cleans the data.
It saves to Google Sheets.
It sends the reply.

All without you doing anything.

n8n has a free cloud plan.
You can get started without spending a single dollar.

Website: n8n.io

---

### Telegram Bot API

This is how the workflow talks to Telegram.

When someone messages your bot,
Telegram sends that data to n8n instantly.

Creating a Telegram bot is completely free.
Takes less than 2 minutes with BotFather.

---

### Google Sheets API

This is where all your leads get saved.

Google Sheets is free.
Your data stays in your own Google account.
You have full control. Always.

You can view leads on your phone, laptop, anywhere.
You can share with your team instantly.
