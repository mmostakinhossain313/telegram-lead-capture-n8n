## Google Sheet Structure

I designed the sheet to be simple.
Clean. Easy to read. Easy to share with your team.

No complicated formulas.
No confusing columns.
Just the data you actually need.

---

### Column Structure

| Column | Name | What It Saves |
|--------|------|---------------|
| A | name | First name of the person who messaged |
| B | user_message | Exactly what they typed |
| C | chat_id | Their unique Telegram ID |
| D | date | Exact date and time of message |

---

### Why This Structure?

I thought about this carefully.

Name — so you know who to follow up with.
Message — so you know what they need before you call.
Chat ID — so you can message them directly on Telegram.
Date — so you know how urgent the follow up is.

Every column has a reason.
Nothing is extra. Nothing is missing.

You can share this sheet with your sales team.
They see the lead. They follow up. Deal closed.

That simple.
